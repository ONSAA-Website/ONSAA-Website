ANNOUNCEMENT

Hi all, 

As I assume most in this class are aware, the Ford government has introduced cuts to OSAP for domestic students, and has uncapped tuition fees for international students. Students Against Austerity, a grassroots student organization that formed to fight Ford's austerity policies, is encouraging students and workers to participate in a strike action to protest the cuts to education on November 5th, 2026. To show my solidarity with this action and encourage participation, I am granting all students in this section an academic exemption for the strike date:

- e.g. Nov 5: lecture moved to online/recorded; pre-class quiz canceled/extended; midterm test postponed to MM/DD

I encourage all of you to participate in this strike and look forward to further actions organized by Students Against Austerity.

EMAIL

Hi [name],

It was great to connect with you at [event name]. We are reaching out to remind you of the student strike we've been organizing for the date of November 5th. We've encountered several students who have been impacted and want to participate, but have expressed concerns about possible detriments to their grades. To encourage student participation in this strike, we're requesting instructors to show their solidarity by granting academic exemptions for the date of November 5th. 

An academic exemption can include holding a lecture online, posting a lecture recording, or postponing/canceling an assessment scheduled for the strike date. If you are interested, you can reply to this email with the following information:

- Institution
- Course code
- Course name
- Description of exemption

This exemption will be added to the Exemption Database on our site, at onsaa.org/exemptions. We encourage participating instructors to announce these exemptions to their students, to make them aware and verify the submissions to student viewing the database. A template for an announcement is available on our site. 

Thank you,
Students Against Austerity
